About

The whole stack.
Solo when it counted.

I spent years as part of a small IT team at Sauk Valley Bank — responsible for every layer of the technology stack in a regulated financial environment. Servers, networking, storage, security, compliance, automation. When something broke, I fixed it. When something needed building, I built it.

That experience shaped a way of thinking that hasn't left me: understand the full system, document everything, and treat security as operational discipline — not an afterthought.

Today I work as a Senior Technical Admin Specialist at Intact Insurance Specialty Solutions, managing on-premises infrastructure while extending my skills into cloud security. I'm currently completing the AZ-500 Microsoft Azure Security Engineer certification and building toward cloud security and OT security roles.

I bring banking-grade discipline to every environment I work in — whether that's a Fortune 500 cloud deployment or a small business that needs to know their M365 tenant isn't wide open.
